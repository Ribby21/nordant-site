How it plays

A shooter played as a sport

Ranked play

Every online match is ranked. Matchmaking looks for players near your level, and if a seat stays empty a bot takes it, so the match still starts.

Squad up

Add friends by their tag, see who's online, and invite them to your party. Parties queue together and always play on the same side.

Trick shots

The Skipstone's bullets bounce off walls. Bank a shot round a corner: a knockout off the bounce is a trick shot, and the stands go wild.

Every round counts

Buy your gear each round with what you've earned: pistols, a shotgun, an SMG, rifles, snipers, armor and grenades. Save when you're short, spend when it counts.
