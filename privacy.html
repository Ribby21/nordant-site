NORDANT Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

NORDANT is a game made by Ribby (ribby.dev). This page says what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. The short version: no ads, no tracking, no selling your data, and no real name or location asked for. An email address is kept only if you choose to sign in with Google.

What the game collects

  • A player account. When you first open the game it makes an anonymous account for you: a random ID, and when it was made and last used. It isn't tied to your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your display name, the name other players see in matches, if you choose one, and your tag (six letters and numbers after it) that friends add you by.
  • Google sign-in, if you use it. Signing in with Google is optional; it lets your account follow you to a new phone. If you do, we keep your Google account's ID and its email address, to link your account to it and show you which account you're signed in with. We don't get your password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Friends and parties. The players you're friends with, friend requests and party invitations you send or receive, and whether you're online or in a match, which your friends can see.
  • Ranked play. Your rating and rank, and the results of the ranked matches you play (who was in them and how they went), to match you with players of a similar level.
  • Your game items: your Nords balance (the game's own currency, earned by playing), the skins you own and wear, and a record of the matches you start and finish, so the game can pay Nords for them.
  • Sign-in tokens, so the game remembers your account. They're kept on your device, and our server keeps only a scrambled (hashed) copy.
  • Connection details. Like any online service, our server and the multiplayer service see your device's IP address when the game connects. Our server's logs record requests, including IP addresses, for security (such as stopping abuse) and fixing problems. They're kept for a limited time and not used for anything else.

What the game doesn't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics or tracking across other apps and websites.
  • No selling or renting of your information to anyone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, microphone, camera or location.

Who else handles it

Only the services the game runs on, and only to run it:

  • Railway hosts our server and its database.
  • Photon (Exit Games) connects players' devices for matches.
  • Edgegap runs the servers ranked matches are played on, and picks one near you from your connection.
  • Google, if you sign in with Google.
  • Unity, the engine the game is built with.

We'd share information otherwise only if the law required it.

Purchases

There's nothing to buy with real money in the game right now. If that changes, payments will go through Google Play or the App Store, and we'll never see your card details. This page will be updated first.

Children

NORDANT isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your account

You can ask us to delete your account and everything kept with it at any time: write to the address below. Uninstalling the game removes its sign-in from your device, but doesn't delete the account on our server.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
