NORDANT Support

Help with the game, from Ribby (ribby.dev).

Get in touch

Found a bug, lost your skins, or have an idea? Write to hello@ribby.dev. Say which phone you play on and what happened, and we'll get back to you.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere on the left half of the screen to move. On the right, run your thumb round the ring of the aim wheel to turn your aim (the line from your gun), and tap FIRE in its middle to shoot; hold it to keep firing. Aim sensitivity in Settings sets how far the ring turns your aim. The tutorial (HOW TO PLAY on the menu) walks you through a round, and the practice range is there to warm up.

What are Nords?

The game's own currency. You earn them by playing matches and spend them in the Bait Shop on skins, which you wear from the Locker. Skins change only how you look.

Deleting your account

Write to the address above and we'll delete your account and everything kept with it. See the privacy policy for what that is.
